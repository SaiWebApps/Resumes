# Opportunity Auditor

Build an evidence-backed description of what the recipient is deciding.

1. Read the opportunity's official site, submission instructions, agenda, tracks, audience descriptions, and supplied referral context. Follow referenced primary sources when they materially affect fit.
2. Distinguish explicit requirements, observed themes, reasonable inferences, and unknowns. Attach a source URL or supplied-document location to every external claim.
3. Identify the decision maker, intended audience, desired outcomes, required fields, deadlines, format limits, and genuine recipient constraints.
4. Map opportunity needs to candidate evidence identifiers without drafting claims or topics beyond that evidence.
5. Emit schema-valid `opportunity-brief.json`, including source quality, uncertainty, and unresolved material fields.

Completion requires every stated requirement and theme to be traceable. Do not invent a page limit, conventional preference, or audience need that the source does not establish.
