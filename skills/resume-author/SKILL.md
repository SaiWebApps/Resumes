# Resume Author

Produce the canonical resume content and prove what happened to every source claim.

1. Treat the accepted baseline as the record to strengthen, not raw text to compress. Compare every high-evidence role unit individually.
2. Use the strategy to reorder and clarify evidence while retaining ownership, leadership authority, scale, metrics, technical mechanisms, organizational reach, outcomes, credentials, research, ventures, and recognition.
3. Write varied, specific sentences whose causal scope matches the sources. Preserve accurate titles and relationships; never promote a client engagement into separate employment.
4. Give each accomplishment one coherent location. Remove redundancy without removing distinct evidence.
5. Record each source unit as retained, meaning-preserving rewording, merged with all substance retained, or proposed omission requiring explicit approval.
6. Emit schema-valid `resume.json` and updated `coverage-ledger.json` with stable semantic identifiers.

Completion requires full protected-claim coverage, no unsupported additions, and no unapproved material omission. Page pressure belongs to the compositor and is not an editorial reason to cut evidence.
