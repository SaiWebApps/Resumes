# Speaker Pitch Author

Connect the candidate's record to the recipient's program decision.

1. Open with the audience problem and the contribution the candidate can make, not a generic application formula.
2. Establish credibility with selected evidence that advances the pitch; let the resume carry the full record.
3. Give each proposed session a distinct question, evidence base, concrete audience outcome, and clear fit with the opportunity.
4. Preserve shared facts and provenance exactly across the resume and letter. Distinguish research findings, production experience, teaching, and inference.
5. Close with format flexibility and a direct program-level conclusion rather than repeating the opening.
6. Emit schema-valid `cover-letter.json` with stable semantic identifiers, the required signature fields, and `proposal_ids` that resolve to the evidence-backed topics in `strategy.json`.

Completion requires every topic claim to map to evidence and every paragraph to serve a distinct persuasive function. Typography and pagination remain compositor responsibilities.
