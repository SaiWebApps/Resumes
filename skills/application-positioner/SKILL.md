# Application Positioner

Choose the narrative architecture before prose or pagination can distort it.

1. Validate that the evidence bundle and opportunity brief are current and conflict-aware.
2. Compare materially different plausible positions against audience relevance, differentiation, evidence strength, breadth preserved, and skeptical-reader objections.
3. Select a primary thesis and supporting credibility arc. Keep the underlying professional record broader than any single proposed topic.
4. Assign each evidence category one coherent home in the resume or pitch outline. Preserve parent-child provenance and prevent duplicate accomplishments from masquerading as additional experience.
5. Define proposed topics with recipient value, evidence identifiers, and concrete attendee outcomes.
6. Emit schema-valid `strategy.json` with the chosen position, rejected alternatives and reasons, resume outline, pitch purpose, required evidence, and protected distinctions.

Completion requires a defensible choice and a complete outline. This stage does not write final sentences, decide typography, or remove evidence to meet layout preferences.
